A ROUTINE I’M STILL BUILDING

Getting stronger, one session at a time

Fitness is something I’m making more room for. I plan my training sessions and repetitions, keep records, and am working toward a more consistent, structured routine. Right now, I’m gradually increasing the weights I use for hip thrusts, shoulder presses, and Bulgarian split squats.
