EXPERIENCE & SKILLS

Brief Introduction

I’m a Biology MSc graduate from the University of Copenhagen, specializing in Molecular Biology and Genetics. I previously earned a BSc in Biotechnology from Nanjing Agricultural University, where my undergraduate research explored bacterial oxidative stress responses through genetic screening and comparative phenotype analysis. My experience spans mammalian cell culture, qPCR, microbiology, and sample preparation and data review for lipid LC-MS analysis. I enjoy troubleshooting unexpected results, maintaining clear experimental records, and making scientific information accessible to different audiences.

I’m seeking opportunities in laboratory research & development, technical support, and quality-related roles, where I can combine hands-on scientific work with careful documentation, problem solving, and clear communication.
