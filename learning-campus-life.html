    SELECTED ACTIVITIES I ORGANIZED

    01 / AN IDEA I PUT INTO PRACTICE

    Monthly Running Challenge

    Initiator & Organizer

    I proposed the idea and developed the programme from the ground up. Participants worked toward a monthly running-distance target, recorded their runs using an app, and submitted check-ins through a shared process. Top-ranked participants received additional rewards.

    I led rule-setting and overall coordination, bringing the participation requirements, check-in process, and reward structure into one plan.

    Programme designRule-settingOverall coordination
    02 / PARTICIPATION & SAFETY

    First Autumn Fun Sports Meet

    Planning & Event Coordination · Undergraduate Years 1–3

    Helped plan the inaugural autumn fun sports meet for first- to third-year students in the College of Life Sciences. I delegated tasks, coordinated venue arrangements, and oversaw event delivery, with a focus on encouraging student participation and maintaining a safe event environment.

    Task delegationVenue coordinationParticipation & safety
    03 / TEAM COORDINATION

    Badminton & Table Tennis Tournaments

    Overall Organizer · Individual Competitions

    Oversaw the organization of individual badminton and table tennis tournaments as Head of the Sports Department. I assigned responsibilities to team members and coordinated preparations and event delivery, checking that each part of the plan was covered.

    Task allocationTeam coordinationAttention to detail
